## 2. Quotes worth using

> "Money was sent and my friend's sibling diverted the funds for something completely ridiculous."

> "I fear to be stolen and to break a relation because of that."

> "My biggest fear is whether the person will carry out the work and if they are trustworthy enough."

> "I'm afraid he won't stick to his commitment and won't build the house properly."

The second one is the best in the set — it names the **relationship** cost, not just the money. That matches the field report's finding that the pain has a shape and the shape is family. No competitor talks about this.

**Asked what's missing:**
- Government / official recognition in the destination country
- A relative doing physical site visits *in addition to* photo monitoring
- Receipts and formal reports at each stage

## 3. Findings that contradict what we built

1. **Photo/video progress proof scored 0%** as most-important feature. It is the thing the product is built around and the centrepiece of the README's verification design. Nobody chose it.
2. **Land title fraud is the single highest-intensity fear** (50% rated it max). We have not built it. One agent bio mentions title verification; there is no feature.
3. **Verified agent profiles won outright at 60%** — this *is* built (directory + profiles). Lead with it.
4. **Currency lock drew 20% unprompted.** The field report independently flagged currency integrity as an underserved "thin atom." Two separate sources now point at the same gap.

## 4. The sample problem — read before quoting any percentage

The product targets **diaspora senders**. The sample largely is not that.

**Where respondents live:** Lagos ×2, Nigeria ×1, Ouagadougou ×4, Burkina Faso ×1, Indiana (US) ×1, Seattle (US) ×1.

**≈7 of 10 live in their home country, not abroad.**

Corroborating signals:
- "How long have you lived abroad?" — **70% answered less than 2 years**
- Only **4/10** currently send money home (2 regularly, 2 occasionally); 5 "not yet but plan to"
- **3/10** answered "not applicable" for who oversees their project
- **0% said fund loss happened to them personally**

**The genuine target segment in this data is roughly 4 people.**

### What this means for the pitch

- **Always state n = 10.** "60%" from six people reads as inflation if the denominator is hidden.
- **Do not call it "diaspora validation" without qualifying it.** If a judge asks who was surveyed and finds mostly Lagos and Ouagadougou residents, the credibility damage is worse than having no survey.
- **Safe framing:** "Ten early responses across Nigeria and Burkina Faso, roughly a third currently abroad. Directional, not statistically significant — here's what it pointed at."
- The **pricing result is the most defensible number** (0/10 rejected the fee) and is least sensitive to the diaspora/home split.
- Owning the sample limitation out loud scores directly against Pillar 6, "awareness of limitations."
